    Short Bio

    I am a researcher working on world models, neural rendering, and simulation systems for robotics and embodied AI, with recent work spanning humanoid world simulators, driving-scene generation, and 3D scene understanding.

    I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from the CAD Research Center, Tongji University, after earning my B.Sc. in Computing Science and Technology from the Department of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Southwest Jiaotong University. I was born in Dengfeng, China—home to Mount Song and a vibrant blend of cultural traditions. A broad STEM foundation, including intensive Biology and Chemistry coursework, shapes how I think about multimodal perception.

    Research Interests

    My research focuses on structured and physically grounded world representations, spanning 3D reconstruction, neural rendering, video generation, and scene understanding. I am especially interested in action-conditioned simulation for humanoid robots and in building representations that support perception, prediction, and interaction within the same world model. If you see an overlap or would like to collaborate, please reach out.

    Current & Past Affiliations

    I am currently at AGIBOT and have also worked with teams at BAAI, Tsinghua University, Shanghai Noematrix, Xiaomi Robot Technology, Megvii, and Tongji University.
